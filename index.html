WHO AM I?

I BUILD THINGS THAT ACTUALLY DO SOMETHING.

I'm a Computer Science student specializing in AI & Machine Learning, with a growing interest in understanding how intelligent systems can be applied to problems that are difficult to model directly. My work sits somewhere between machine learning, research, and software engineering — from building backend and data systems to experimenting with state-space models, neural networks, and anomaly detection.

I'm particularly interested in problems where the data is noisy, incomplete, or where we don't fully know what we're looking for. That has led me toward projects involving sensor reliability and forecasting, as well as a broader research project exploring how multiple astronomical data sources can be combined to identify unusual patterns that individual systems might miss. I enjoy taking open-ended problems, breaking them into measurable pieces, and building systems that can actually test the ideas.

03 / PROJECT RECOMMENDATION SYSTEMS

MOVIE
MATCH

Content-Based Movie Recommendation App

MovieMatch helps users discover films from a TMDB-derived catalog. A FastAPI service powers a browser interface for searching titles, choosing a movie, reviewing personalized recommendations, and opening full movie details.

RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

  • TF-IDF features built from plot overviews, taglines, keywords, genres, directors, cast, and collections
  • Content similarity combined with genre preference fit and Bayesian quality scoring
  • Weighted ranking with a 200-candidate retrieval stage
  • Recommendation results expose content, genre, quality, and final scores

APP & API

  • FastAPI endpoints for movie search, lookup, recommendations, and details
  • Search and lookup by TMDB ID or exact title
  • Optional genre preferences and configurable recommendation count
  • Movie details include genres, director, cast, runtime, ratings, and links
